# One Advisory: Prior Art and Differentiation

| Existing capability | We acknowledge it | Our bounded contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Advisory-zone management | UOMS and public systems define affected geography | Start from the authorized zone, never claim it |
| Critical-customer lists | Utilities can identify vulnerable and institutional customers | Turn three facility classes into different operational tasks |
| Mass notification | Mature systems send and log warnings | Verify acknowledgement, evidence, assistance, and recovery |
| Approval and audit | Existing platforms retain approval and message history | Apply human gates to task delivery and resource conflicts |
| Rescission | Authorities and platforms publish all-clear notices | Coordinate facility-specific recovery after authorized rescission |

The contribution is not “AI sends boil-water notices.” It is a source-bearing, cross-facility
completion loop with a visible policy rejection and a human-resolved scarce-resource conflict.

No scoped search proves global uniqueness. Public language therefore says “our contribution,” not
“world’s first.”
