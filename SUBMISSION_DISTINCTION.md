# Cross-submission distinction

One Advisory is the Fortified Enterprise Fleet entry. Its primary object is an authorized water advisory and its work is differentiated institutional response across dialysis, school/childcare and long-term care. Its defining architecture is managed discovery, runtime identities, gateway governance, Model Armor and human resource authority.

It does not share ColdClock's medicine, professional-review, pharmacy or delivery workflow. It does not share Plan Kept's participant-controlled response privacy or promise-to-reality synthesis. Reused wake, verification, quarantine and observability primitives are disclosed infrastructure; the fleet, schema, UI, model evaluation and submission narrative are independent.
