# External validation protocol

Do not invent users, quotes, or outcomes. Run one 25-minute synthetic tabletop with an emergency manager, water-utility operator, public-health preparedness lead, or critical-facility administrator.

Ask the participant to follow advisory ingestion through facility response, conflict, escalation, and recovery. Record role only, completion time, wrong turns, whether advisory/allocation authority was unmistakable, and whether the managed-agent receipts helped audit the response. Obtain permission before publishing quotes.

Acceptance target: no mistaken belief that AI issued/rescinded the advisory or allocated water, all three facilities accounted for, and recovery status understood without developer intervention.
